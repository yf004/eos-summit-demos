
1. `button-demo.py`
	single click: displays click
	double click: displays double click
	long press (700 ms): displays long press
	returns to hi! after 1.5s
2. `counter.py`
	click: increments counter by 1
	long press: resets counter to 0
3. `timer.py`
	click: starts or pauses the timer
	long press: resets timer to zero and pauses it
4. `ball-box.py`
	click: pauses or resumes movement
	long press: resets the ball to a random position and direction
	ball continuously bounces off the screen edges
5. `ball-bounce.py`
	click: drops the ball from a new random position and height
	ball bounces with decreasing energy until it stops
6. `reaction.py`
	first click: starts the game
	click while red: ends the round (too early!)
	click when green: records reaction time in milliseconds
	click after result: starts a new round
